Forge Runner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 October 2026

Forge Runner ("the game", "we", "us") is a free browser game made in Western Australia. This policy explains what information the game uses when you play at forgerunnergames.com (including www. and runner.). Questions: hello@forgerunnergames.com.

If you play Forge Runner on another website (for example Poki or CrazyGames), that website's privacy policy applies to your visit there.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-up, and we never ask for your email or real name.
  • The game stores a few settings on your device, such as your best score and whether sound is on.
  • It sends us anonymous play events (for example "a run started", "a run ended with score 742") so we can see whether the game is fun. These use a random ID, not your name, and we don't store your IP address with them.
  • There are no ads on this website.

What we collect and why

Stored on your device (browser localStorage), never sent to us unless listed below: - best score (forge-runner-hi), sound setting (fr_snd2) - a random player ID (forge-runner-pid, 16 random characters) - the campaign tags of the link you first arrived from, if any (forge-runner-utm) - the optional name you type when sharing a challenge (forge-runner-name). It only goes into the share link you choose to send. - your 3-character leaderboard initials (forge-runner-initials)

You can clear these at any time by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Sent to us (play events): when you visit, start a run, finish a run or share, the game sends: - the random player ID - the event type, your score, the run length and the time since the page loaded - whether you arrived from a challenge link - the campaign tags (utm_source / utm_campaign / utm_content) of the link you came from - your country (a 2-letter code such as NZ), which our hosting provider works out from your connection. We store only the country with play stats, never your IP address

We use this only to count players and improve the game. It is stored in our server logs (Amazon Web Services, Sydney region) for 30 days and then deleted automatically. We don't keep access logs, and we don't store your IP address or browser details anywhere. Like any website, our hosting providers (Amazon CloudFront and AWS Lambda) handle your IP address to deliver the page, under their own security practices.

Leaderboard: if you submit a score, we store your 3-character initials, your best score, how long the run lasted, your country and the random player ID so the score can appear on the public leaderboard. We keep your best score until you ask us to remove it; email us to remove an entry.

We do not sell your information, and we don't use it to build advertising profiles.

Children

Forge Runner is a general-audience game that anyone can enjoy. We don't knowingly collect personal information from children. The game has no chat, no accounts and no profiles, and the only text a player can type (a share name, or 3-character leaderboard initials) is optional. If you are a parent or guardian and think your child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your browser's site data to delete everything stored on your device.
  • Email us to ask what we hold, to correct it or to delete it (for example a leaderboard entry). Because play events use a random ID, include your player ID if you can (we can tell you how to find it).
  • If you are in Australia, you can also contact the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner (www.oaic.gov.au). If you are in the EEA or UK, you may contact your local data-protection authority.

Where data is stored

Our servers are in Sydney, Australia (AWS ap-southeast-2) and on Amazon CloudFront's global network.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top. Significant changes will be shown in the game.
